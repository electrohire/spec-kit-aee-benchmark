# Program ledger

The benchmarking program's token ledger lives in two artifacts with two different jobs. This arrangement was settled on 2026-10-26 and is stated here so that neither artifact is ever mistaken for the whole ledger.

* The founding record is `benchmarks/program-ledger.jsonl` in this repository. It carries the ledger's six founding entries — the recorded chain of **23,237,750 tokens**, **52,654,187 tokens**, **53,954,521 tokens**, **54,717,194 tokens**, **54,947,139 tokens** and **56,952,934 tokens** — and it is frozen: the H-1 tests in `tests/test_h1_ledger.py` pin exactly that state, no campaign close is ever appended to it, and those tests are not amended to make room for one.
* The ledger of record for campaign closes is the program's working ledger, kept with the benchmarking program's own records outside this repository as `program-ledger-closed.jsonl` (SHA-256 `7292da2bf69b43ce28857d2808aec953be4318633684c2f80b2716de53117ce1`). It carries the same six founding entries and then one close per campaign, under the H-1 admission rule that a campaign opens only at the ledger's last close. Its closed state has **8 entries** and a final position of **60,895,857 tokens** of the **64,700,000 token** program cap, with the **500,000 token** reserve untouched.

The split exists because the two jobs pull in opposite directions. The founding record's value is that it cannot change: it is the evidence that the spending chain began exactly as recorded, and its tests prove that on every run. Campaign closes are append-only by nature and keep arriving as campaigns run. Freezing the founding artifact and appending closes to the working ledger keeps both properties, and the chain is unbroken across the split: the working ledger's first campaign close opens at the founding record's last close of **56,952,934 tokens**, and its hash chain covers all **8 entries**.

Each artifact verifies independently with the repository's own ledger code, `verify_ledger` and `metric_audit` in `src/benchmark_runner/ledger.py`: the founding record at **6 entries** with last close **56,952,934 tokens**, the working ledger at **8 entries** with last close **60,895,857 tokens**. A pointer file beside the working ledger names this note as the governing statement of the arrangement.
